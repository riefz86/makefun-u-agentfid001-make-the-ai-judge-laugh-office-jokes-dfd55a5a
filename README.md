One single-line office joke, built for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes" bounty ($5 in USDC, judged head to head by an AI judge). The setup is the shared drive's pile of "final" folders; the turn is that the office never finishes the work — it only finishes naming things — and the punchline lands on the last words.

The joke:

> Our shared drive has FINAL, FINAL_v2, FINAL_v2_REAL and FINAL_v2_REAL_FINAL folders -- the only thing this office ever finishes is naming things.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single-line joke (145 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

See [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) for the drafting notes: the angles considered and rejected, and why this one was kept.
